    • Q7552

      Nov. 20, 2026

      What does "also" mean?
      “Also” means that the effect given by that effect has the same duration and targets as that effect's previously resolved portion.
      For example, in the case of “Until the end of the turn, all of your XX Digimon gain AA. If you have a Tamer, they also gain BB,” all of your XX Digimon gain AA until the end of the turn, and if you have a Tamer, all of your XX Digimon also gain BB until the end of the turn as well.

    Card Q&A

    • Q7559

      Nov. 20, 2026

      What does "isn't affected by [...] effects" mean, exactly?
      This effect prevents a card from being affected by effects.
      For example, your Digimon won't suspend if it's chosen for a "suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon" effect, and its DP won't be reduced by 3000 if it's chosen for a "1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -3000 DP" effect.

    • Q7560

      Nov. 20, 2026

      Can a card that has an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect be chosen for an effect?
      Yes, it can be chosen.
      For example, a Digimon that isn't affected by effects can be chosen for a "suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon" effect.

    • Q7561

      Nov. 20, 2026

      Can a card that has an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect be given an effect?
      Yes, it can.
      It won't be affected by it, but it can be given an effect.
      However, if an effect such as is given to a Digimon that isn't affected by effects, the Digimon won't be considered to have that effect.

    • Q7562

      Nov. 20, 2026

      If a card is affected by an effect, then it later gains an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect, what happens to the effect that was affecting it?
      As soon as it gains the "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect, it will no longer be affected.

    • Q7563

      Nov. 20, 2026

      If a card has an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect, it gains an effect, then it later loses the "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect, what happens to the effect that it gained?
      It will be affected by the effect as soon as it can be affected by effects.

    • Q7564

      Nov. 20, 2026

      A card that has an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect was given an effect that triggers at a timing such as [When Attacking].
      Will the effect trigger if that card later meets the trigger conditions?
      If the Digimon isn't affected by effects upon the trigger timing, the effect won't trigger.

    Card Q&A

    • Q7565

      Nov. 20, 2026

      What does an "add X000 to the maximum your DP-based deletion effects can delete" effect do?
      It increases the maximum value described in "delete Digimon with DP X000 or less" effects.
      For example, while "[Your Turn] Add 1000 to the maximum DP you can choose with DP-based deletion effects" is active, "[When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 3000 DP or less" will allow you to delete an opponent's Digimon with 4000 DP or less.

    • Q7566

      Nov. 20, 2026

      If "add X000 to the maximum your DP-based deletion effects can delete" is active, can the maximum be increased for an effect such as "delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with DP less than or equal to this Digimon's DP" that references a Digimon's DP for deletion?
      No, it can't be increased.
      You can't increase the maximum of a DP-based deletion effect that doesn't show a numerical value.

    • Q7570

      Nov. 20, 2026

      I currently have 1 [Kanata Hondo] when I attack with this Digimon and delete my opponent's Digimon in battle.
      At this time, if an effect on my opponent's deleted Digimon removes that [Kanata Hondo] from the battle area, can this card perform a security check with the gained from its [Your Turn] effect?
      Yes.
      In this case, the and the effect on your opponent's Digimon that was deleted in battle trigger simultaneously. The turn player's effects are activated first, so activates and effects that perform checks before End of Attack timing will be pending.
      Even if the opponent's deleted Digimon's effect activates afterward and your Digimon loses due to [Kanata Hondo] being removed from the battle area, resolving pending effects that perform checks before End of Attack timing means the security check will be performed at that time.
